About Us

We bridge buyers, suppliers, and logistics partners across borders.

Lange International Trade Services is a Papua New Guinean owned intermediary agency focused on reliable international trade facilitation between Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, and China.

Founded by Brandon Kiangali, BA International Trade & Economics, MBA, the company brings together academic knowledge, China-based exposure, supplier networks, and practical trade coordination experience.

What We Do

Practical services for businesses that want to trade with confidence.
