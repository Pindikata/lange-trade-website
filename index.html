About the Company

Built to reduce risk and make international trade easier.

Lange International Trade Services is a Papua New Guinean owned intermediary agency focused on trade facilitation between Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, and China.

Founded by Brandon Kiangali, BA International Trade & Economics, MBA, the company combines academic training, China-based exposure, supplier networks, and practical trade experience to help clients move from inquiry to delivery with greater confidence.

Core Services

Focused services for serious buyers and growing businesses.

01

Supplier Sourcing

Identify suitable manufacturers and suppliers based on product requirements, target pricing, minimum order quantity, and client expectations.

02

Supplier Verification

Reduce unnecessary risk by checking supplier background, communication reliability, product fit, and basic business credibility before serious commitment.

03

Negotiation Support

Support price discussions, product specifications, sample arrangements, payment terms, and order communication between buyer and supplier.

04

Logistics Coordination

Coordinate with logistics partners for shipment planning, freight options, packing details, and delivery support from China to the Pacific.
