Who We Are

A practical bridge between China’s supply base and business demand across the Pacific.

Lange International Trade Services is an international trading and sourcing company focused on helping businesses in Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands and the wider Pacific access reliable products, manufacturers and supply solutions from China.

We combine supplier sourcing, procurement support, commercial negotiation, order coordination and logistics planning into one streamlined service, giving clients clearer visibility from enquiry to delivery.

Mission

Simplify cross-border trade.

Provide dependable sourcing, procurement and trading support built on transparency, responsiveness and sound commercial judgment.

Vision

Become a trusted Pacific-facing trade partner.

Link businesses across the region with competitive, reliable and scalable supply opportunities across China and beyond.

What We Do

End-to-end commercial support for buyers accessing China’s manufacturing and supply market.

01

Product Sourcing & Supplier Identification

Search, shortlist and coordinate with suitable manufacturers and suppliers based on specifications, target price, quantity and delivery requirements.

02

Procurement & Commercial Negotiation

Support price negotiation, MOQs, payment terms, specifications, packaging requirements and commercial details before order confirmation.

03

Import & Export Trading

Support legitimate cross-border trade transactions, including direct trading structures and import/export coordination where appropriate to the order.

04

Order & Quality Coordination

Follow production schedules, samples, specifications, packaging and quality requirements, and coordinate inspections or verification where needed.

05

Custom Product & Manufacturing Support

Coordinate custom-made products, private-label requirements, packaging adjustments and factory communication for non-standard purchasing projects.

06

Logistics & Delivery Coordination

Work with qualified logistics partners to plan shipment methods, documentation, consolidation and delivery based on cost, urgency and destination requirements.

Business Model

Flexible transaction structures built around the order and client requirement.

Lange is designed to support both agency-style sourcing and direct trading transactions. The appropriate structure depends on the product, order value, supplier arrangement and agreed commercial terms.

Sourcing / Agency

Commercial support without unnecessary complexity.

Lange identifies suppliers, negotiates terms, coordinates specifications and supports execution while the purchasing arrangement follows the client’s agreed structure.

Direct Trading

Trading structures where appropriate.

Lange can purchase from qualified suppliers and supply goods to customers under agreed commercial terms where a direct trading structure is suitable.

Logistics Coordination

Qualified partners for physical movement.

Shipping and delivery are coordinated through freight, customs and logistics partners selected for the product, destination and Incoterms. Lange does not position itself as the physical carrier.
