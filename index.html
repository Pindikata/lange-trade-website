Company Overview

Bridging markets. Building trust. Creating value.

Lange International Trade Services is a premier trade facilitation agency specializing in seamless and trustworthy international trade between Papua New Guinea, the Solomon Islands, and China. Our work extends beyond these core markets, making us a versatile partner for global trade transactions.

Founded by Brandon Kiangali, BA International Trade & Economics, MBA, the company leverages deep knowledge of international economics and a growing network in China to help businesses move with clarity and confidence.

Our Mission

To simplify cross-border trade processes while empowering businesses to unlock their full growth potential through sourcing, negotiation, logistics coordination, and strategic consulting.
